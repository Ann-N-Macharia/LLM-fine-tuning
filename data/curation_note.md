# Curation Note — Kenya Legal Aid Dataset (100 examples)

## Sourcing
Every example is grounded in the primary text of Kenyan statutes, retrieved from Kenya Law's official portal (kenyalaw.org / new.kenyalaw.org — the National Council for Law Reporting), cross-checked against the Judiciary of Kenya and government sources where the statute text alone was ambiguous. Core statutes consulted: Employment Act 2007 ([kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2007/11](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2007/11); ss.5, 6, 10, 19, 28, 29, 35, 37, 40, 41, 44, 45, 56), Land Act 2012 (s.79 spousal consent), Land Registration Act 2012 ([s.26 certificate of title](https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/4131)), Law of Succession Act ([kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1972/14](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1972/14); ss.35, 36, 38, 40), Matrimonial Property Act 2013 (s.7), Community Land Act 2016, Rent Restriction Act Cap 296 and Landlord and Tenant (Shops, Hotels and Catering Establishments) Act Cap 301 ([kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1965/13](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1965/13)), Small Claims Court Act 2016 (as amended 2020, Kshs 1,000,000 threshold), Legal Aid Act 2016 ([nlas.go.ke](https://www.nlas.go.ke), s.35(2)), Work Injury Benefits Act 2007, NSSF Act 2013, Employment and Labour Relations Court Act 2011 and Environment and Land Court Act 2011 ([judiciary.go.ke](https://judiciary.go.ke)), and the Limitation of Actions Act. Scenarios were then hand-written to apply each provision to realistic ordinary-Kenyan situations.

## Quality and safety criteria
Every answer follows context → cited Act/section → plain-language explanation → exceptions → concrete next step. No answer states a legal outcome the statute doesn't support: where a figure or procedure is not currently verifiable (e.g. exact overtime rates, filing fees), the answer says so explicitly rather than inventing a number. Every answer carries a disclaimer directing the reader to a qualified advocate or a named legal-aid provider (NLAS, FIDA Kenya, Kituo Cha Sheria), and none gives a binding legal judgment.

## Coverage gaps
No county-specific practice (by design, scoped to national statutes); no criminal procedure beyond basic assault reporting; case-law nuances (e.g. constructive dismissal, succession-act constitutional challenges) are flagged as evolving rather than settled; regulations/wage orders with frequently-revised figures are deliberately left unquantified.
